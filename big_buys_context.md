# Big Buys: Company Context

Applies to: **Big Buys Data Model** → **Final Combined**

## About Big Buys

Big Buys is a U.S. consumer electronics retailer headquartered in Columbus, Ohio, founded in 2009. It sells TVs, computers, audio, cameras and smart-home products through its stores.

## Calendar

- The fiscal year runs **February 1 to January 31**. FY2026 = Feb 1, 2026 to Jan 31, 2027.
- Fiscal quarters: Q1 = Feb–Apr, Q2 = May–Jul, Q3 = Aug–Oct, Q4 = Nov–Jan.
- "This year", "last quarter" and "YTD" always mean **fiscal** periods unless the user says "calendar".

## Glossary

- **Basket**: an order (one Order Number).
- **Flagship / Standard / Express**: the three store formats in Store Tier, largest to smallest. Always sort by **Store Tier Order**, never alphabetically.
- **Members**: customers enrolled in the **Big Buys Rewards** loyalty program (the Loyalty Program column).
- **Merchant**: the internal buyer responsible for a product (Merchant Id).
- **Premium**: the top Product Tier.

## Rules

- Compare stores only **within the same Store Tier**. A Flagship vs. an Express store is not a fair comparison.
- Show blank Age Group or Cust Gender as **"Unknown"**. Never guess.
- Do not show **Customer Name** next to Age Group or Cust Gender.
- **Store Open Date** is placeholder data. Do not use it. Say it is not yet available.
- Product reviews and star ratings are **not** in this data model.
- There is no returns, inventory or store traffic data.
